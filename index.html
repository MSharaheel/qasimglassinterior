Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products and services

Do you deal in both local and imported products?

+

Yes, we offer both local and imported glass, aluminum, and interior design products. Our imported range includes premium quality materials from international brands, while our local products are sourced from the best manufacturers in Pakistan.

What types of wall panels do you offer?

+

We offer a wide range of wall panels including WPC (Wood Plastic Composite), PVC, Fluted panels, PS Solid panels, PU panels, and UV Marble sheets. Each type comes in multiple designs, colors, and finishes.

Do you provide installation services?

+

Yes, we provide complete installation services for all our products. Our experienced team ensures professional installation with attention to detail and quality craftsmanship.

How can I place an order?

+

You can place an order through our WhatsApp at +92 3034348982, visit our shop at Ghazi Road Lahore Cantt, or contact us at +92 303 4348982. You can also use the "Order on WhatsApp" button on any product page.

What is your shop location?

+

Our shop is located at Ghazi Road Lahore Cantt, Opposite Punjab Housing Society Naka, Lahore, Pakistan. You are welcome to visit us during business hours.

Do you offer sound proof and double glazed windows?

+

Yes, we specialize in sound proof glass aluminum windows and double glazed glass aluminum windows. These are ideal for noise reduction and energy efficiency. Both local and imported options are available.
