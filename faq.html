Everything you need to know about our products and services

What products do you offer?

+

We offer a comprehensive range including glass aluminum windows, doors, partitions, double glazed windows, sound proof windows, tinted glass, mirror walls, standing mirrors (with and without LED), wall mirrors, and all kinds of wall panels including WPC, PVC, Fluted, PS Solid, PU panels, and UV marble sheets.

Do you deal in both local and imported products?

+

Yes, we deal in both local and imported products. Our imported range includes premium quality materials from international brands, while our local products are sourced from the best manufacturers in Pakistan. You can filter products by type on our products page.

What types of wall panels do you offer?

+

We offer WPC (Wood Plastic Composite), PVC, Fluted panels, PS Solid panels, PU panels, and UV Marble sheets. Each type comes in multiple designs, colors, and finishes suitable for both residential and commercial spaces.

Do you provide installation services?

+

Yes, we provide complete professional installation services for all our products. Our experienced team ensures perfect installation with attention to every detail and quality craftsmanship.

Do you offer sound proof and double glazed windows?

+

Yes, we specialize in sound proof glass aluminum windows and double glazed glass aluminum windows. These are ideal for noise reduction and energy efficiency. Both local and imported options are available.

What types of mirrors do you offer?

+

We offer standing mirrors (with and without LED), wall mirrors (with and without LED), mirror walls for full wall coverage, and decorative mirrors. All available in various sizes and designs.
