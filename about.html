Welcome to Qasim Glass Aluminum & Interior Design

We are one of Lahore's leading glass aluminum and interior design companies, providing premium quality products and services for residential and commercial projects. Our shop, located at Ghazi Road Lahore Cantt, has been serving customers with excellence and dedication.

We specialize in glass aluminum windows, doors, partitions, double glazed and sound proof windows, tinted glass, mirrors (with and without LED), and a comprehensive range of wall panels including WPC, PVC, Fluted, PS Solid, PU panels, and UV marble sheets.

What sets us apart is our commitment to quality. We deal in both local and imported products, giving our customers the freedom to choose based on their preferences and budget. Our team of experienced professionals ensures flawless installation and complete customer satisfaction.
